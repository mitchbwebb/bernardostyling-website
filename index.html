Gil do Vigor na festa de 60 anos da Globo

Styled by Bernardo

Styling de Bernardo destaque em um dos eventos de moda mais prestigiados do Brasil

Credito da Foto: Gil do Vigor na festa de 60 anos da Globo - Foto: Divulgacao / GQ Brasil

For the special occasion, Gil opted for a designer look valued at R$ 18,300. In an exclusive interview with GQ Brazil, he shares details of his choice, made under the supervision of his personal stylist, Eddy Bernardo. "I wanted something elegant, refined, discreet, but that, at the same time, represented the grandeur of this event, which deserved a look chosen with great care." Gil wears a full Armani look (R$ 11,650) and Dolce & Gabbana shoes (R$ 6,700).
